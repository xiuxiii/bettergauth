Key Concept
Independence of perpendicular motion
Vertical motion under gravity sets the time; horizontal motion sets the distance. They don't interfere.
The key move: even though the horizontal push is unusual, the vertical and horizontal motions are still independent. Gravity alone sets the fall time.
So start with part (a) only — which equation links the 80 m drop to the time in the air?
